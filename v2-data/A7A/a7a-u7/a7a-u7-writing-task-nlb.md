## 范文:  
> Hello, my name is Mike. Today is my 14th birthday. My family have a wonderful dinner at noon. We have hamburgers, pizzas and lots of fruit and vegetables. I also drink cola after dinner. My father buys a toy car for me and my mother gives me a pair of shoes. I like them very much.  
> After that, I play basketball for half an hour with my friends. We have a great time together. This is a happy day.

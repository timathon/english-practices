Unit 1 You and Me · Section B 课堂实录与教学用语脚本

❓ BIG Question: What do we need to know about a new friend?
课型：读写结合与综合实践课
时长：45 分钟 (1 课时)
核心产出：交友回帖 & 个人档案制作 (Profile)
拓展阅读：Reading Plus 交友三大建议 (p.75)
标准课堂用语标识图例： Greeting 问候导入 Directive 课堂指令 CFU 理解检查 Praise 表扬反馈 Transition 环节过渡 📝 Student Task 学生活动
Step 1: Warm-Up, Lead-In & Photo Board Brainstorming 00:00 - 06:00 (6 mins)
[00:00 - 02:00] Daily Greeting & Thematic Hook
L01
T: Greeting "Good morning, class! Welcome back to our English classroom. How is everyone feeling today?"
L02
Ss: "Good morning, teacher! We are feeling great!"
L03
T: Praise "Wonderful energy! In Section A, we learned how to greet each other, ask for names, classes, and teachers. But suppose you want to make a deeper, long-lasting friendship. Just knowing a name is not enough, right?"
L04
Ss: "Right! Not enough!"
L05
T: Transition "Exactly! That brings us to our core question today: 'What do we need to know about a new friend?' What details do you want to find out?"
L06
S1: "Their hobbies!"
L07
S2: "Their age and favourite food!"
L08
S3: "Do they have pets? Where are they from?"
L09
T: Praise "Brilliant ideas! 10 points for Group 1 and Group 2! We want to know their hobbies, favourites, pets, and families."
*[Teacher writes the title on the blackboard: Unit 1 You and Me · Section B: What do we need to know about a new friend? and sets up the 4-Group PK table: Group 1: 10 | Group 2: 10 | Group 3: 0 | Group 4: 0]*
[02:00 - 06:00] Photo Board Exploration (1a p.24) & Word Box Labelling
L10
T: Directive "Please open your textbooks to page 24. Look at activity 1a. Here we have two photo boards, Board A and Board B, and a word box with 7 key expressions."
L11
T: Directive "Let's read the expressions in the word box together. Ready, go!"
L12
Ss (in chorus): "Parrot! Beijing roast duck! The UK! Singapore! Guitar! The Great Wall! Tennis!"
L13
T: 📝 Student Task "Excellent pronunciation! Now, spend 1 minute looking at the photos and labelling each photo with the correct word from the box. Work individually. Go!"
*[Students work on page 24. Teacher walks around the classroom monitoring and providing guidance.]*
L14
T: Transition "Time is up! Let's check our answers. S4, what is photo 1 on Board A?"
L15
S4: "Photo 1 is Singapore! And photo 2 is a parrot."
L16
T: CFU "Spot on! And what about photos 3 and 4 on Board A, S5?"
L17
S5: "Photo 3 is tennis, and photo 4 shows a big family."
L18
T: Praise "Terrific! Now on Board B: photos 5, 6, and 7. Who can label them all? S6, please."
L19
S6: "Photo 5 is the UK and the Great Wall, photo 6 is Beijing roast duck, and photo 7 is a guitar!"
L20
T: Praise "Awesome job! 10 points for Group 3 and Group 4! Now, look at these photo boards. What can you predict about these two students before we even read their posts?"
L21
S7: "Student A might live in Singapore, love tennis and have a parrot! Student B might come from the UK, love Chinese food, and play the guitar in a band!"
L22
T: Praise "What sharp detective eyes! Let's read their online posts to verify our predictions!"
Step 2: Reading & Information Mapping 06:00 - 18:00 (12 mins)
[06:00 - 09:00] Skimming & Photo Board Matching (1b p.24)
L23
T: Directive "Look at 1b on page 24. Here are two forum posts under the title 'Making new friends'. One post is from Pauline Lee, and the other is from Peter Brown."
L24
T: 📝 Student Task "Please skim both posts quickly in 1.5 minutes and match each student with their photo board. Ready? Set, go!"
*[Students read silently and match Pauline with Board A, and Peter with Board B.]*
L25
T: CFU "Time is up! Who matches Board A? All together, please!"
L26
Ss: "Pauline Lee!"
L27
T: CFU "And who matches Board B?"
L28
Ss: "Peter Brown!"
L29
T: Praise "Correct! Our predictions were 100% accurate!"
[09:00 - 14:00] Scanning & Information Matrix Completion (1c p.25)
L30
T: Directive "Now, let's scan for detailed information. Turn to page 25, activity 1c. Look at the table. There are 7 dimensions: Age, Country, Family, Pet, Favourite food, Favourite place, Hobby."
L31
T: 📝 Student Task "Read the two posts carefully and complete the table with key words and phrases. You have 3 minutes. Underline the evidence in the text as you write."
*[Students scan the texts and fill in the information matrix. Teacher draws the mindmap and table structure on the blackboard.]*
L32
T: Transition "Let's check our information matrix together! Group 1, please tell us about Pauline's Age and Country."
L33
S8 (G1): "Pauline is 14 years old. She lives in Singapore."
L34
T: CFU "Great! Group 2, what about her Family, Pet, and Hobby?"
L35
S9 (G2): "She lives with her big family. Her pet is a parrot named Coco. Her favourite sport is tennis, and she often plays it after school."
L36
T: Praise "Excellent! Now let's examine Peter Brown. Group 3, tell us Peter's Age, Country, and Family."
L37
S10 (G3): "Peter is 13 years old. He is from London, the UK, but now lives in Beijing with his family."
L38
T: CFU "Spot on! And Group 4, what are Peter's favourites and hobbies?"
L39
S11 (G4): "His favourite food is Beijing roast duck, his favourite place is the Great Wall, and he likes music and plays the guitar in the school band!"
L40
T: Praise "Terrific job, everyone! 10 points to every group! Let's write these key points on our board mindmap."
*[Teacher notes down Column 1 (Information Dimensions) on the blackboard:
1. Information Dimensions
● Basic Info: Name, Age, Country | ● Family & Pet: big family / parrot (Coco) / cat (Toby)
● Favourites: Food: Beijing roast duck | Sport: tennis, basketball | Place: the Great Wall
● Hobby: play the guitar in school band]*
[14:00 - 18:00] Language In-Depth & Critical Discourse Analysis
L41
T: Directive "Look at two fascinating sentences in the text. First, in Pauline's post: 'She even speaks some Chinese!' Notice the adverb even /'i:vn/. What emotion does it express here?"
L42
S12: "Surprise! It shows Coco the parrot is super smart and amazing!"
L43
T: Praise "Exactly! It highlights something surprising and impressive. Now look at the endings of both posts. Pauline asks: 'Do you want to be my friend?' and Peter asks: 'Would you like to be my friend?'"
L44
T: CFU "Compare these two questions: which one sounds more polite and gentle?"
L45
Ss: "'Would you like to be my friend?'"
L46
T: Praise "Spot on! 'Would you like to...' is a polite modal invitation. And when replying, we say: 'I'd like to be your friend.' Remember: I'd = I would."
L47
T: Directive "Also note the musical phrase: 'play the guitar'. When playing musical instruments, we always use the definite article the (play the guitar, play the piano), but for sports, no article: 'play tennis, play basketball'. Let's read these together!"
L48
Ss (in chorus): "Play the guitar! Play tennis! Would you like to be my friend? I'd like to be your friend!"
Step 3: Comparison & Writing Scaffold 18:00 - 26:00 (8 mins)
[18:00 - 22:00] Text-to-Self Comparison (1d p.25)
L49
T: Transition "Now that we know Pauline and Peter so well, let's build a bridge from the text to ourselves! Look at activity 1d on page 25: 'Compare either Pauline or Peter with yourself.'"
L50
T: Directive "Choose one person: Pauline or Peter. What do you have in common? What is different? Take 2 minutes to fill in the comparison chart in your notebook."
*[Students fill in their comparison notes. Teacher invites two students to share orally.]*
L51
T: CFU "S13, who did you compare yourself with?"
L52
S13: "I compared myself with Peter! Peter is 13 years old, and I am 13 years old too. Peter plays the guitar, but I play the piano. We both live in Beijing and love Beijing roast duck!"
L53
T: Praise "Terrific! Look at how S13 used connecting words like 'too', 'but', and 'both'! 10 points for Group 1!"
[22:00 - 26:00] Sentence Structure Scaffolding (2a p.25)
L54
T: Directive "Now look at activity 2a on page 25. Here are the core sentence patterns you will need to describe yourself:
1. My name is...
2. I'm ... years old.
3. I live in ... (with my ...)
4. I love / like ...
5. My favourite ... is ..."
L55
T: 📝 Student Task "Turn to your shoulder partner. Practice introducing yourself using these 5 sentence patterns. 1 minute each. Go!"
*[Pair work oral practice. Students speak energetically with their partners. Teacher writes Column 2 (Writing Scaffold) on the blackboard:
2. Writing Scaffold (Profile)
• My name is ... / I'm ... years old.
• I live in ... with my ...
• My favourite ... is ...
• I'd like to be your friend. / Would you like to be my friend?
• Connectors: and, but, too, also, even]*
Step 4: Guided Writing & Peer Feedback 26:00 - 34:00 (8 mins)
[26:00 - 30:00] Writing the Reply Post (2b p.25)
L56
T: Transition "Now, imagine you are logged into the school online community. You see Pauline's and Peter's posts looking for friends. It's time to write a warm, polite reply post!"
L57
T: Directive "Look at activity 2b: RE: Making new friends. Note the structure:
• Salutation: Hello, Pauline / Peter!
• Self-introduction: Name, age, city.
• Common Ground & Favourites: I'd like to be your friend. I love ... too. My favourite ... is ...
• Closing Invitation: Please write to me."
L58
T: 📝 Student Task "Please pick one student and complete your reply post on page 25. Make sure your handwriting is neat and clear. You have 4 minutes. Start writing!"
*[Students write their replies individually. Teacher circulates, giving on-the-spot support for spelling and grammar.]*
[30:00 - 34:00] Peer Assessment & Live Showcase
L59
T: Directive "Pens down! Exchange your post with your partner. Check your partner's writing against our 3-Star Checklist on the screen:
⭐ Content: Complete personal information & shared interests?
⭐ Grammar: Correct *be* verbs and spelling?
⭐ Tone & Format: Polite invitation (*I'd like to... Please write to me.*)?"
*[Students do peer editing. Teacher selects a high-quality sample from S14 to display under the document visualizer.]*
L60
T: Directive "Let's look at S14's reply on the screen. S14, please read your post to the class."
L61
S14 (reading proudly): "Hello, Peter! My name is Li Ming. I'm 13 years old. I live in Chengdu with my parents. I'd like to be your friend. I love music too, and I play the violin. My favourite food is hot pot. Please write to me."
L62
T: Praise "Terrific job, Li Ming! Complete information, perfect grammar, natural connection (*I love music too*), and beautiful handwriting! 3 stars and 10 points for Group 2!"
Step 5: Project: Personal Profile & "Guess Who" Game 34:00 - 42:00 (8 mins)
[34:00 - 38:00] Model Analysis (Andre Kalu 3b p.26) & Profile Making
L63
T: Directive "Now turn to page 26. Let's look at our Unit Project: 'Make your own personal profile'. First, let's read the example profile written by Andre Kalu."
L64
Ss (in chorus): "My name is Andre Kalu. I'm 13 years old. I'm from South Africa, but now I live in China. I live with my father, my mother, and my brother. We have a pet cat. His name is Toby. My favourite food is beef noodles. My favourite sport is basketball. I play basketball every weekend with my brother and my friends."
L65
T: CFU "Look at how Andre structured his profile:
1. Name & Age & Origin (South Africa -> China)
2. Family & Pet (cat Toby)
3. Favourites & Routine (beef noodles, basketball every weekend)"
L66
T: 📝 Student Task "Now take your colored Personal Profile Card. Write your own profile following this structure. Secret Rule: Do NOT write your real name at the top; write a secret code number on the back instead! You have 3 minutes."
*[Students write their profile cards enthusiastically. Teacher collects the profile cards into a mystery box.]*
[38:00 - 42:00] Interactive Guessing Game: "Guess Who He/She Is!" (3c p.26)
L67
T: Transition "Here comes our exciting game: 'Guess Who He or She Is!' I will draw a card from the mystery box, read the clues, and the fastest group to guess the student's name wins 10 points!"
L68
T: Directive "Listen carefully: 'This student is 12 years old. She lives in Beijing with her parents and two dogs. Her favourite sport is badminton. Her favourite place is the Science Museum. She likes drawing cartoons after school.' Who is she?"
L69
S15 (raising hand quickly): "Is she Zhang Yuanyuan from Group 3?"
L70
T: CFU "Yuanyuan, is that your card?"
L71
Zhang Yuanyuan (smiling): "Yes! That's me!"
L72
T: Praise "Bingo! 10 points for Group 4! Let's do one more. S16, come up and draw a card to describe to the class."
L73
S16 (reading card): "He is 13 years old. He loves playing basketball and his favourite food is Beijing roast duck. He plays the drums in the school band!"
L74
Ss (all shouting enthusiastically): "Wang Junkai! It's Wang Junkai!"
L75
T: Praise "Spot on! You really know each other so well now!"
Step 6: Summary, Reading Plus Extension & Homework 42:00 - 45:00 (3 mins)
[42:00 - 44:00] Thematic Synthesis & Reading Plus Wisdom (p.75)
L76
T: Transition "What a productive lesson! Today we explored all dimensions of knowing a new friend: basic info, family, pets, favourites, and hobbies."
L77
T: Directive "To wrap up Unit 1, let's open to page 75, Reading Plus: 'Making New Friends at School'. What are the 3 golden pieces of advice for making friends?"
L78
Ss: "1. Don't be shy! 2. Ask questions! 3. Don't forget to share!"
L79
T: Praise "Exactly! Remember: Getting to know each other is the first step in making new friends. Be brave, be a good listener, and share your wonderful world!"
*[Teacher writes Column 3 (Reading Plus & PK) on the blackboard:
3. Reading Plus & PK
★ 3 Pieces of Advice (p.75): 1. Don't be shy! (Smile & talk) | 2. Ask questions! (Good listener) | 3. Share! (Interesting things)
★ Group PK Final: G1: 50 | G2: 50 | G3: 50 | G4: 50]*
L80
T: Praise "Let's check the final group scores: Group 1 has 50 points, Group 2 has 50 points, Group 3 has 50 points, and Group 4 has 50 points! A four-way champion tie! Let's give everyone a huge round of applause!"
L81
Ss: (Applause and cheering) "Yay!"
L82
T: Directive "Here is your homework for today:
1. Polishing: Polish your Personal Profile card, paste a photo or draw a self-portrait, and post it on our classroom Friendship Wall.
2. Reading: Read the full passage of Reading Plus on page 75 and complete the 2 comprehension tasks.
3. Extension: Make a short self-introduction video or audio (1 minute) to share with a partner."
L83
T: Greeting "Class is over. Thank you, boys and girls! Have a wonderful day!"
L84
Ss: "Thank you, teacher! Goodbye!"

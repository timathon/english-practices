"No," Damao smiles (微笑). "Da isn't my last name and Mao isn't, either (也)."

"Really?" asks Lisa Green, an American girl. "Then what's your last name?"

"Han, and my full name is Han Damao," Damao says.

"It's fun," Tony and Lisa say.

Then they talk about their teacher. "Who is in our classroom?" Damao asks.

"Helen Keller," Tony says. "She is our English teacher from America."

"Can I call her Miss Helen?" Damao asks.

"No, please call her Miss Keller. Helen is her first name," Tony says.

"Oh, I see. Thank you, Tony," Damao says. Then Damao and his friends go into the classroom happily (快乐地).

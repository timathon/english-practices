Unit 1 You and Me · Section A 课堂实录与教学用语脚本

❓ BIG Question: How do we get to know each other?
课型：听说与语法综合课
时长：45 分钟 (1 课时)
核心语法：be 动词一般现在时 & 姓名文化
标准课堂用语标识图例： Greeting 问候导入 Directive 课堂指令 CFU 理解检查 Praise 表扬反馈 Transition 环节过渡 📝 Student Task 学生活动
Step 1: Warm-Up, Greeting & Thematic Lead-In 00:00 - 05:00 (5 mins)
[00:00 - 02:00] Daily Greeting & Hook
L01
T: Greeting "Good morning, boys and girls! Welcome to our junior high English class. Welcome to Grade 7! I'm Mr Liu, your English teacher."
L02
Ss: "Good morning, Mr Liu!"
L03
T: Greeting "How is everyone doing today? Are you excited to start our new journey?"
L04
Ss: "Yes! Very excited!"
L05
T: Greeting "Here we are! A brand new school, a brand new journey, so many lovely new faces! And around us, there must be lots of new... (pauses with an expectant smile, waiting for students to chime in)"
L06
Ss (smiling & shouting out): "New classrooms! New books! New teachers!"
L07
T: Praise "Right! Of course, new teachers and new books! But most of all, what are they? Yes, NEW FRIENDS! Look around the classroom—every classmate sitting beside you could become your best friend!"
L08
T: Transition "So, here comes our BIG Question for this unit: '❓ BIG Question: How do we make new friends?'"
*[Teacher writes the title on the blackboard: Unit 1 You and Me, notes down the Big Question, and sets up the 4-Group PK Points table: Group 1 | Group 2 | Group 3 | Group 4]*
[02:00 - 05:00] Photo Exploration (p.19) & 1a Prior Knowledge Activation
*[Teacher clicks PPT to project the photograph on page 19 showing Chinese middle school students wearing light-yellow school uniforms and red scarves, talking and gesturing warmly in a school hallway / corridor, holding folders/notebooks.]*
L09
T: Directive "Now, eyes on the screen, please. Look at the photograph on page 19 and let's explore the three 'Look and share' questions together!"
L10
T: CFU "Question 1: Who are the people in the photo? Look at their uniforms and red scarves. Amy, please."
L11
Amy: "They are middle school students / Grade 7 students. They are classmates."
L12
T: Praise "Spot on, Amy! 10 points for Group 1! Question 2: Where are they? Are they inside a classroom, on the sports field, or somewhere else? David?"
L13
David: "They are in the school corridor / hallway outside their classroom. Look, there is a photo wall behind them."
L14
T: Praise "Great observation! They are in the bright hallway. Now, Question 3: How do you think they feel?"
L15
S2 (Leo): "They are smiling. They feel happy, friendly, and excited to meet new classmates!"
L16
T: Praise "Terrific! Look at their warm smiles and confident eyes. They are making new friends just like us on the first day of school!"
L17
T: CFU "Here is a bonus question: What do you think the girl on the right is doing? Look at her hand gesture."
L18
S3 (Grace): "She is welcoming a new student, or introducing her friend to the other classmates!"
L19
T: Transition "That's right! 10 points for Group 2! She is introducing a friend. Let's see how we introduce ourselves and get to know each other in Section A: 'How do we get to know each other?'"
*[Teacher writes Section A subtitle on the board: Section A: How do we get to know each other?]*
L20
T: Directive "Now, please open your books to page 20. Look at Activity 1a. Here is a list of expressions. Please take 30 seconds to tick the expressions you already know. Ready? Go!"
*[Teacher walks around the aisles, observing students' ticking speed, then claps hands twice to regain attention.]*
L21
T: CFU "Time is up! Who would like to read out two expressions you ticked? Leo, please."
L22
Leo: "Hello. Good morning. Nice to meet you."
L23
T: Praise "Excellent pronunciation! 10 points for Group 1! Let's say 'Nice to meet you' to your partner. Turn left, turn right, and shake hands!"
L24
Ss (in pairs): 📝 Student Task "Nice to meet you!" "Nice to meet you too!"
Step 2: Listening Input, Information Mapping & Pronunciation 05:00 - 15:00 (10 mins)
[05:00 - 08:00] 1b Picture Ordering & 1c Detail Catching
L25
T: Transition "It seems some of you are not quite familiar with expressions like 'What class are you in?' or 'Who's your class teacher?'. Don't worry! Let's listen to 1b and see how our textbook friends use them to introduce themselves. Look at the pictures in 1b."
L26
T: Directive "You will hear two conversations. Listen carefully and number the pictures in the order you hear them. First listening, pens ready!"
*[Teacher plays the audio for 1b. Students listen and write numbers in the boxes.]*
L27
T: CFU "Who can share your order? Look at the two pictures—one shows a teacher and a student, and the other shows a boy and a girl. David, please."
L28
David: "Conversation 1 is the right picture (the boy and girl, Peter and Meimei) as Number 1, and Conversation 2 is the left picture (Ms Li and Ella) as Number 2."
L29
T: Praise "Bingo! Perfect answer, David! 10 points for Group 2! Now let's challenge ourselves with 1c. Look at items 1 to 5. We need to catch: Full name, Country, Class, Last name, and Age. Listen again and circle the coloured words you hear."
*[Teacher plays audio 1c. After playing, Teacher writes key prompts on board.]*
L30
T: CFU "Let's check together! To get to know someone, we ask: Name (What's Peter's full name?), Country (Where is he from?), and Class & Age (What class and how old is Ella?)."
L31
Ss: "Peter Brown from the UK, in Class 1! Ella Miller is 12!"
*[Teacher adds 4 inquiry branches to the board mindmap: 🌿 Name (May I have your name?) · Country (Where are you from?) · Class & Grade (What class are you in?) · Age (How old are you?)]*
L32
T: Directive "Great! 10 points for Group 2 & 3! Now check 2e on page 22. Look at rows 1-3: Peter (UK, Class 1), Song Meimei (China, Class 1), Ella (Ella Miller, US). Fill them in! Peter's class teacher is blank—we'll find out in our next dialogue!"
[08:00 - 15:00] Pronunciation Focus: Front Vowels /i:/, /ɪ/, /e/, /æ/ & Contractions
L33
T: Transition "Now turn back to page 21. Look at /i:/, /ɪ/, /e/, /æ/. These are Front Vowels (前元音): keep your tongue tip touching your lower front teeth! As mouth opens wider (/i:/ smile wide → /æ/ 2 fingers wide), the tongue front gradually lowers."
*[Teacher models minimal pairs: feet vs. fit, men vs. man. Students place fingers on jaw to check openness.]*
L34
T: CFU "Listen & show fingers: 'man' (/æ/) = 2 fingers, 'men' (/e/) = 1 finger. Ready? 'Men'!"
L35
Ss: *[Hold up 1 finger]* "/e/!"
L36
T: Directive "Spot on! In Activity 2, notice contractions: I'm, What's, We're. In daily spoken English, always use contractions to sound natural and smooth!"
Step 3: Dialogue Exploration & Cultural Etiquette 15:00 - 25:00 (10 mins)
[15:00 - 19:00] 2a Dialogue Reading & Comprehension
L37
T: Directive "Look at the picture in 2a on page 21. A man looking like a teacher is standing on the left, and on the right, a girl is gesturing with her hand while a boy scratches his head. What are they talking about? Let's listen to the dialogue and circle the coloured words."
*[Teacher plays audio 2a. Students listen, follow the lines, and circle the coloured words.]*
L38
T: CFU "First, which four coloured words did you circle in the dialogue? Who can share?"
L39
Ss: "They are good, friend, What, and Oh, sorry!"
L40
T: CFU "Exactly right! 10 points for Group 1! Now let's check the table in 2c on page 22. Where is Mr Smith from, and what is his job?"
L41
S4: "Mr Smith is from the US. His job is English teacher."
L42
T: Praise "Correct! What class is Peter in, and who is his class teacher?"
L43
S5: "Peter is in Class 1. His class teacher is Ms Gao."
L44
T: Directive "Great! Now we can finish the table in 2e on page 22! Write down 'Ms Gao' for Peter's class teacher and 'China' for Chen Jie. Look, our 4 student profiles are 100% complete! Let's also add this new inquiry question to our board mindmap: 'Who's your class teacher?'."
*[Teacher adds branch to the board mindmap: 🌿 Teacher / Class Teacher → Who's your class teacher? (Ms Gao. / Mr Lu.)]*
[19:00 - 25:00] Cultural Focus: Western vs. Chinese Names & Error Correction
L45
T: CFU "Wait a second! Why did Chen Jie stop Peter and say: 'Oh, Peter, it's Ms Gao, not Ms Hui. Hui is her first name.'? Why did Peter say 'My mistake'?"
L46
S6: "Because Peter called the teacher by her first name Hui, but we should use her last name Gao!"
L47
T: Praise "Brilliant explanation, Kevin! Give a round of applause for Kevin!"
*[Teacher draws a comparison diagram on the chalkboard:
Western Name: Peter (First) Brown (Last) → Mr Smith
Chinese Name: Gao (Last/Family) Hui (First) → Ms Gao (✔) / Ms Hui (✘)]*
L48
T: Directive 📝 Student Task "In English-speaking culture, when we address teachers or elders politely, the golden rule is: Title + Last Name. So Gao Hui is Ms Gao, and Smith is Mr Smith. Now, 1 minute for pair role-play: One is Chen Jie, one is Mr Smith, one is Peter. Practice with real expressions!"
Step 4: Grammar Deduction & Rule Chant (be verbs) 25:00 - 35:00 (10 mins)
[25:00 - 29:00] 3a Grammar Observation & Chant
L49
T: Transition "Now let's turn to Grammar Focus on page 23. Let's look at the magical 'be-verbs': am, is, are."
L50
T: Directive "Look at the sentences in 3a. Read them quietly and circle the be-verbs. Then match them with the pronouns: I, You, He/She/It, We/They."
*[Students observe and fill in blanks. Teacher draws the be-verbs Singular & Plural table on the blackboard:
📊 be-verbs (Simple Present):
• Singular (单数): I + am (I'm) | You (你) + are | He / She / It / Ms Gao + is (He's / It's)
• Plural (复数): We / You (你们) / They / Emma and Ella + are (We're / They're)]*
L51
T: CFU "Let's chant together to remember the golden grammar rule! Clap your hands with me!"
*[Teacher leads rhythmic clapping chant:]*
"我用 am，你用 are，is 连着他她它；
单数名词用 is，复数名词全用 are！"
L52
T: Directive "Let's practice the question form! When we make a question, what happens to the be-verb? It jumps to the front! 'You are Peter' → 'Are you Peter?' 'Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.'"
[29:00 - 35:00] 3b & 3c Application Practice
L53
T: Directive "Now, apply this rule to 3b on page 23. Pay special attention to item 1: 'Emma and Ella'—is it singular or plural? And item 5: 'Tom and I'—which be-verb do we use? You have 2 minutes to complete 3b and 3c."
*[Teacher walks the room, checks answers, and invites S7 and S8 to write 3b answers on the board.]*
L54
T: CFU "Look at item 5: 'Tom and I are both in the school band.' Why 'are', not 'am'?"
L55
S7: "Because 'Tom and I' means two people, it's plural (we), so we must use 'are'!"
L56
T: Praise "Clear as crystal! Super job! Group 4 gets 10 points!"
Step 5: Integrated Role-Play & Communication (1d & 3d) 35:00 - 42:00 (7 mins)
[35:00 - 42:00] 1d & 3d "Speed Friending & Introducing Classmates" Role-Play
L57
T: Directive "Now comes our exciting communication challenge combining 1d (page 20) and 3d (page 23): 'Speed Friending & Introducing Friends!'"
L58
T: Directive 📝 Student Task "Two tasks in groups of 4:
Task 1 (1d on page 20): Mingle and get to know your group members using: 'May I have your name?' / 'How do you spell your name?' / 'Are you...?'
Task 2 (3d on page 23): Use the 3d speech frame to introduce your new partner to another pair:
👉 'Hello! I'm... This is my new friend. His/Her name is... He's/She's from... His/Her favourite... is...'
You have 4 minutes! Go!"
*[Active classroom buzz: Students practice 1d inquiry and 3d friend introductions in groups. Teacher circulates, monitoring pronunciation and pronoun usage.]*
L59
T: Directive "Time is up! Who would like to present your 3d friend introduction to the whole class? Group 3, Tina, please!"
L60
Tina: "Hello, everyone! I'm Tina. This is my new friend. His name is Zhang Wei. He's from Beijing, China. We are in Class 1, Grade 7. His favourite sport is basketball. We are good friends!"
L61
T: Praise "What a wonderful 3d introduction, Tina! Accurate pronouns, lively delivery! 20 points for Group 3!"
Step 6: Summary, Thematic Wrap-Up & Homework 42:00 - 45:00 (3 mins)
[42:00 - 45:00] Value Consolidation & Homework Assignment
L62
T: Transition "Look at our board. Let's count our points: Group 2 and Group 4 are our champion groups today with 50 points! But every group did an outstanding job."
L63
T: CFU "Today we learned how to ask names, spell names, identify teacher titles, and use be-verbs. Remember our golden thought: 'Getting to know each other is the first step in making new friends.' Don't hesitate to smile and say 'Hello' to everyone around you!"
L64
T: Directive "Here is our homework for today:
1. Complete the After-Class Practice Sheet (Page 3 of our learning guide).
2. Complete the Section B preview task: Read the vocabulary on page 24 (parrot, guitar, tennis) and look at the photo boards.
Class is over. Thank you, boys and girls! Have a wonderful day!"
L65
Ss: "Thank you, teacher! Goodbye!"

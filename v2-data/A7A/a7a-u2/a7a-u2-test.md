阅读表达(每小题4分,共20分)
阅读短文,根据短文内容回答问题。

Everyone has a family. It is the place where life begins and love never ends. In English, "FAMILY" can be "Father and mother, I love you." In Chinese, "家" looks like a house—the "宀" looks like a roof (屋顶) and the "豕" means "pig".

Why is there a "pig" under the "roof"? In old times, many families needed pigs to be their food. No food, no life. So pigs really meant a lot to a family. Now, some Chinese people give "家" a new story. The two strokes (笔画) under the roof are grandparents. They keep the family members together. Other members are under the grandparents. On their right are the parents. They work hard and teach their children. On their left are the children. Brothers and sisters have fun together.

There are so many different families, but in all the families, people love and help each other. "A happy family makes everything fine." What is your happy family like?

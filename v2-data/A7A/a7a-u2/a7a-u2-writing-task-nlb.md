## 范文:  
> Dear Dave,  
> How are you? I want to show you a photo of my family.  
> This is my grandfather. He is 65 years old. And this is my grandmother. She is 64 years old. They are at home. These are my parents. My mother's name is Zhang Li. She is a teacher. She teaches English in a high school. This is my father. He is a doctor. He works in a hospital. His name is Li Wei. They are both 35 years old. Look! The girl in the photo is me.  
> I have a happy family. Can you tell me something about yours?  
> Yours,  
> Li Juan

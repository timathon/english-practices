**Writing Task: My Favourite School Day**

Write a short passage about your favourite school day. Describe your schedule and subjects.

<!-- col -->

**Details to include:**  
- Daily schedule and timing  
- School subjects you have  
- Which subject is your favourite and why  
- How you feel about the day

<!-- col -->

**Requirements:**  
- Mention at least 4 school subjects.  
- Use connectors like "and", "but", and "because".  
- Word count: 60-80 words.

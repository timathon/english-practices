## 范文:  
> ### My Favourite Subject
> My favourite subject is English because it's an interesting language. It's useful, too. I like it and I want to speak English well. Our English teacher, Mrs. Li is great. All the students in our class like her and her lessons because her lessons are always easy and interesting. I like talking with her in English after class.

Step 3: Listening Comprehension (15:00 - 25:00)
[15:00 - 20:00] Listening for Ingredients (Activity 1b)
L19 T: Transition & Pacing Now that we know how to describe actions, let's listen to Peter and Teng Fei making mashed potatoes. Look at Activity 1b on page 42. Instruction & Directives Listen to the conversation and tick the ingredients you hear. Are you ready? Let's listen!

[20:00 - 25:00] Numbering Instructions (Activity 1c) & Pronunciation Focus
L23 T: Instruction & Directives Now, listen again for Activity 1c. Number the instructions in the correct order from 1 to 6. Let's play the audio again.
[T plays audio a second time. Students write numbers in boxes.]
L24 T: Let's check the sequence. What is step 1? S5?
L25 Student 5 (S5): Wash the potatoes and cut them into pieces.
L26 T: Encouragement & Feedback Fantastic! The full order on the board: 1-Wash & cut, 2-Boil until soft, 3-Get butter & milk, 4-Mash potatoes, 5-Add milk & butter and mix, 6-Add salt and pepper.
[T updates blackboard writing with the sequence mindmap.]
[Blackboard Writing: Mashed Potatoes Sequence: First (Wash & cut) -> Next (Boil) -> Then (Get butter/milk & mash) -> Next (Add milk/butter) -> Finally (Add salt & pepper)]
Step 4: Speaking & Pronunciation Workshop (25:00 - 33:00)
[25:00 - 29:00] Pronunciation Sounds & Intonation (Page 43)
L27 T: Instruction & Directives Please turn to page 43. Let's look at the pronunciation table. Notice diphthongs like /əʊ/ in potato and /aʊ/ in house, as well as consonant clusters like /sk/, /st/, /sp/, /str/. Listen and repeat after the recording.
[T plays pronunciation audio. Students listen and repeat chorally.]
L28 T: Listen to the intonation and pauses in Activity 2: To make a banana milkshake... First, peel the bananas. Then, cut up the bananas. Notice the pause marked with double bars ||. Excellent rhythm, everyone!
[29:00 - 33:00] Pair Speaking Practice (Activity 1d & 2d)
L29 T: Student Task Now, let's practice speaking! Look at Activity 1d and 2d. Work with your elbow partner. One student asks "How do we make mashed potatoes / steamed fish?", and the other answers using sequential adverbs: First... Next... Then... Finally... You have 3 minutes. Go!
[T walks around the room, coaching pairs on intonation and vocabulary usage.]
L30 T: Stop, please! Let's invite a pair to role-play in front of the class. Group 4 volunteers? S6 and S7, please!
L31 S6 & S7: [Standing up]
S6: How do we make stir-fried eggs and tomatoes?
S7: First, cut the tomatoes into small pieces. Next, mix the eggs in a bowl...
L32 T: Encouragement & Feedback Wow, what a brilliant performance! Loud and clear! 15 points for Group 4! Let's give them a big hand.
Step 5: Grammar Focus & Exclamatory Sentences (33:00 - 40:00)
[33:00 - 37:00] Analyzing Exclamatory Sentences (Page 45)
L33 T: Instruction & Directives Please turn to page 45. Let's study the Grammar Focus. Look at how we express strong feelings using exclamatory sentences with What and How.
[T writes grammar formulas on the blackboard clearly.]
[Blackboard Writing: Grammar Focus: Exclamatory Sentences
1. What + (a/an) + adj. + noun (+ subject + verb)!
Ex: What an amazing smell it is! / What a mess!
2. How + adj. / adv. + subject + verb!
Ex: How delicious the dish looks! / How clever you are!]
L34 T: CFU - Checks for Understanding Notice the structure! With a countable singular noun, we use What a/an. With adjectives alone, we use How. Let's check Activity 3b. Number 1: "It's a pretty hat." How do we change it with "what"? S8?
L35 Student 8 (S8): What a pretty hat it is!
L36 T: Encouragement & Feedback Spot on! What a pretty hat (it is)! Number 2: "The weather is terrible" with "how"? S9?
L37 Student 9 (S9): How terrible the weather is!
L38 T: Encouragement & Feedback Brilliant! How terrible the weather is! You've mastered the grammar rule.
[37:00 - 40:00] Completing Conversations (Activity 3c)
L39 T: Student Task Complete Activity 3c using the Word Bank expressions in 2 minutes. Go!
[T reviews student answers quickly across the room.]
L40 T: Let's check: 1. C (What a lovely cake!), 2. B (What a surprise!), 3. E (How time flies!), 4. D & F. Excellent work!

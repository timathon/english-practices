Unit 5 What a Delicious Meal! · Section B Instructional Design

❓ BIG Question: Why do we learn to cook? / What does cooking give us?
Class Type: Reading & Vocabulary/Writing
Duration: 1 Lesson (45 mins)
Grade Level: Grade 7 / Middle School
Textbook: Pages 46–49 & 87

语言能力 (Language Ability)

Master target vocabulary (*memory, visible, pumpkin, cinnamon, Thanksgiving, couple*) and adverbs of sequence; comprehend a reflective text about food, memory, and love.

文化意识 (Cultural Awareness)

Explore cross-cultural culinary traditions (pumpkin pie, Thanksgiving, Guoqiao rice noodles, pizza history); understand that cooking embodies love and cultural connection.

思维品质 (Thinking Qualities)

Infer main ideas, analyze personal reflections on food memories, sequence multi-step processes logically, and critically connect personal experiences to cultural narratives.

学习能力 (Learning Ability)

Develop reading comprehension strategies (skimming, scanning, summarizing) and collaborative recipe-writing skills through structured peer interaction.

Teaching Focus: Reading comprehension of "Food for Thought", mastering countable/uncountable nouns and measure words in recipes, writing a personal dish post.
Difficult Points: Appreciating figurative language (*"Cooking is love made visible"*), differentiating abstract memory vocabulary, and structuring cohesive paragraph writing using sequential adverbs.
Methods & Aids: Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT), Communicative Language Teaching (CLT), Multimedia PPT, Reading Graphic Organizers.

Step 1: Warm-up & Lead-in
(5 mins)		 1. Show mouth-watering food photos (e.g., pumpkin pie, apple pie, homemade noodles).
2. Ask guiding questions: "What's your favourite dish? Who often makes it for you? What kind of food makes you happy?"
3. Elicit initial emotional connections to food. 		1. Look at the visual prompts and share personal favorite dishes in pairs.
2. Discuss emotionally resonant food experiences with classmates. 		Activate prior knowledge, build affective engagement, and transition smoothly into the unit's emotional core.
Step 2: Pre-reading & Fast Reading
(8 mins)		 1. Direct students to Page 46 (Activity 1b): "Read the text 'Food for Thought' and tick the best main idea."
2. Monitor reading and guide students to scan for overarching themes.
3. Check answers and explain key stylistic tone (reflective essay). 		1. Read the passage independently using skimming techniques.
2. Select the best main idea from the three options.
3. Share findings and justify choices based on paragraph 1 & final remarks. 		Train global reading skills, train students to identify thesis statements and main ideas quickly.
一、教学详案 - 续表 (Detailed Lesson Plan - Steps 3 to 6)
教学环节与时间 教师活动 (Teacher Steps & Sample Scripts) 学生活动 (Student Activities) 设计意图 (Design Rationale)
Step 3: Careful Reading & Deep Inquiry
(12 mins)		 1. Assign Activity 1c (detailed questions about the grandmother, pumpkin pie history, and kitchen atmosphere).
2. Guide students through Activity 1d (recipe picture ordering) and Activity 1e (text summary completion).
3. Highlight cultural takeaways: "How does cooking help us connect with others and other cultures?" 		1. Reread the text carefully to extract specific details.
2. Answer comprehension questions, order pie-making pictures, and fill in summary blanks.
3. Discuss grandmother's cultural openness. 		Develop close reading, information extraction, and critical thinking regarding cross-cultural friendship and family legacy.
Step 4: Vocabulary in Use & Grammar Scaffold
(8 mins)		 1. Lead Vocabulary in Use (p. 48): Review countable/uncountable food nouns and measure words (*a bottle of milk, a slice of bread*).
2. Guide students through apple pie recipe completion (Activity 2c) using box phrases (*add... to, mix... with*).
3. Analyze Guoqiao Rice Noodles cultural post (p. 49). 		1. Categorize food items and match pictures with measure word phrases.
2. Complete the apple pie recipe blanks.
3. Circle ingredients used in the Yunnan rice noodles post and note sequence words. 		Reinforce lexical items, measure word usage, and recipe structure mechanics in contextualized practice.
Step 5: Writing Workshop & Peer Transfer
(7 mins)		 1. Prompt students to complete notes for their favourite dish (Activity 3b: ingredients, reasons, steps).
2. Guide students to write a social media post response (Activity 3c) following the model: "Hi! My favourite dish is... Here's how to make it. First..."
3. Facilitate peer editing. 		1. Draft personal favorite dish descriptions using graphic notes.
2. Write a 40–50 word recipe post.
3. Exchange drafts with a partner to check for sequential adverbs and clarity. 		Scaffold output writing from structured notes to authentic communicative blog post generation.
Step 6: Summary, Reflection & Homework
(5 mins)		 1. Summarize key takeaways: "Cooking is love made visible. It connects people across cultures."
2. Review reflecting questions (p. 50).
3. Assign homework: Polish the dish post and prepare for the unit project (Recipe creation). 		1. Reflect on emotional and cultural values of cooking.
2. Note down homework requirements and preview upcoming project tasks. 		Consolidate emotional values, reinforce lesson objectives, and guide autonomous after-class learning.

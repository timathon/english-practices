**A**

Food is a window to different cultures. When I studied in Thailand last year, I learned to cook Tom Yum Goong (冬阴功汤). The process taught me more than just cooking skills.

First, my host mom helped me prepare some ingredients- lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves, and galangal. Haha! I'd never heard of them before. "Cooking is like painting," she said. "You must balance different flavors(味道)-sour, sweet, salty, and spicy."

Next, we added the shrimp (虾) and poured coconut milk in. The soup was boiling and filled the kitchen with a wonderful smell. When it was ready, we ate it with sticky rice. The mixture of flavors danced on my tongue.

My host family explained that Tom Yum Goong is more than a dish. It's a symbol of Thai <u>hospitality</u>. They cook it for guests to show warmth and respect. Through this experience, I realized that every culture expresses its stories and values through food.

Now I cook Tom Yum Goong for my family in China. It helps me think of my time in Thailand and share Thai culture with others. Food truly connects people across borders (国界).

Exploring foreign food allows us to discover new worlds. Even simple dishes can tell rich cultural stories. You don't always need to travel far-try a new restaurant in your city. The flavors might be different, but the feelings they create are common. Food builds bridges between people and nations. That's the magic of cooking tradition.

The Green Rice Ball, known as "aiban"(艾粄) in southern China, is a sweet traditional Chinese snack. It's mainly eaten on the Qingming Festival. It's a popular food in the Hakka area. Let's learn to make "aiban".

**Materials we need:** wormwood leaves (艾叶), glutinous rice flour (糯米粉), sugar, water, and red bean filling.

**Making procedure:**

[*LAYOUT: A sequence of six numbered procedural steps arranged with icons and arrows indicating the recipe order.*]

- ① Wash the wormwood leaves carefully.
- ② Boil them in hot water for about 10 minutes.
- ③ Put them into the blender and beat them.
- ④ Mix the glutinous rice flour with the wormwood juice. Add water and sugar to make a dough.
- ⑤ Divide the dough into small balls.
- ⑥ Add some red bean filling in the middle and wrap(包) it up like a little ball.
- ⑦ Steam the "aiban" in a steamer for about 15 minutes.

Wormwood is a herb that is good for our health. It can help us stay strong and out of sickness. And the glutinous rice flour gives us energy, which is important for growing teenagers like us. Why not go and make "aiban" now?

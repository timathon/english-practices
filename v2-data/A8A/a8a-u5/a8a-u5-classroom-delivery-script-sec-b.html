Classroom Delivery Script: Unit 5 Section B (Grade 7 English)

Unit 5: What a Delicious Meal! · Section B 课堂实录与教学用语脚本

❓ BIG Question: Why do we learn to cook? / What does cooking give us?
Grade Level: Grade 7 English (七年级英语)
Duration: 45 minutes (1 class period)
Class Type: Reading & Writing Integration Lesson
Core Competencies: Food & Memory, Reading Comprehension, Adverbs of Sequence, Writing a Food Post

Standard Classroom English Indicators

Greeting & Hook Instruction & Directives CFU (Checks for Understanding) Encouragement & Feedback Transition & Pacing Student Task
Step 1: Warm-Up & Lead-In (00:00 - 07:00)
[00:00 - 03:00] Daily Greeting & Unit Re-entry | [03:00 - 07:00] Discussing Favorite Dishes & Memory Hook
[T enters the classroom, smiles warmly, scans the room, and gestures for students to settle down.]
L01 T: Greeting & Hook Good morning, boys and girls! Welcome back to our English class. How are you doing today?
L02 Ss: Good morning, teacher! We're fine, thank you! And you?
L03 T: Encouragement & Feedback I'm wonderful, thank you! Please sit down. 10 points for Group 3 for your energetic greeting!
[T writes the Lesson Title and Big Question on the blackboard.]
Board: Unit 5 What a Delicious Meal! | Big Question: Why do we learn to cook?
L04 T: Instruction & Directives Now, class, look at the screen. Last session, we learned how to make mashed potatoes and stir-fried eggs and tomatoes. Today, we are moving on to Section B: What does cooking give us? Let's look at the Big Question on the board: Why do we learn to cook? Keep this in mind as we explore today's lesson.
L05 T: Student Task Let's start with Activity 1a on page 46. Work in pairs. Look at the two questions: 1. What's your favourite dish? Who often makes the dish for you? 2. What kind of food makes you happy? Talk with your partner for two minutes. Go!
[T walks around the classroom, monitoring pair discussions, listening to S1 and S2 talk about dumplings and chicken soup.]
L06 T: Transition & Pacing Time is up. Pens down, eyes on me, please. S1, what's your favourite dish?
L07 S1: My favourite dish is tomato beef soup. My grandmother often makes it for me.
L08 T: Encouragement & Feedback Spot on! That's a great dish. 10 points for Group 2! Food is not just for eating; it connects us to our family members and our memories.
Step 2: Pre-Reading & Fast Reading (07:00 - 18:00)
[07:00 - 10:00] Predicting Main Idea (1b) | [10:00 - 18:00] Intensive Reading & Text Comprehension ("Food for Thought")
L09 T: Instruction & Directives Now, let's turn to page 46, look at the passage titled "Food for Thought". Before we read every word, let's do Activity 1b. Read the three options for the main idea and tick the best one. You have one minute to skim the text. Ready? Go!
[T observes students skimming the text, tracking who is reading actively.]
L10 T: CFU (Checks for Understanding) S2, which option did you tick? Let's hear your choice.
L11 S2: I ticked the first one: "Through food and cooking we create memories and show love to others."
L12 T: Encouragement & Feedback Brilliant! That is absolutely correct. 10 points for Group 4.
L13 T: Instruction & Directives Now, let's read the text paragraph by paragraph together. Please follow along with your finger or pen.
[T reads Paragraph 1 aloud with expressive intonation, pausing at key vocabulary words: "visible", "memories", "connect us".]
L14 T: CFU (Checks for Understanding) Look at the first sentence: "Cooking is love made visible." What does the grandmother mean by this? S3, can you explain?
L15 S3: It means love is an idea, but when you cook, people can see it, taste it, and feel it.
L16 T: Encouragement & Feedback Outstanding explanation! You expressed that so deeply. 15 points for Group 1!
[T writes key vocabulary mindmap on the blackboard.]
Board Mindmap: Cooking = Love made visible | Key Words: memory, visible, cinnamon, pumpkin pie, host family, secret
L17 T: Instruction & Directives Let's continue to Paragraph 2 and 3. Notice the writer's favorite memory: her grandmother's pumpkin pie, made in autumn when the weather got colder. And what smell reminds the writer of her grandmother? S4?
L18 S4: The smell of cinnamon!
L19 T: Encouragement & Feedback Spot on! Cinnamon. Excellent. Let's look at the Pumpkin Pie Recipe box on page 46. Let's review the ingredients: eggs, sugar, cinnamon, salt, pumpkin, cream, pie crust.
Step 3: Post-Reading & Text Analysis (18:00 - 27:00)
[18:00 - 23:00] Answering Comprehension Questions (1c) | [23:00 - 27:00] Sequencing Recipe Pictures (1d) & Summary Completion (1e)
L20 T: Instruction & Directives Now, look at Activity 1c on page 47. There are 5 questions about the text. Work with your group members, discuss the answers, and write them down in your notebooks. You have four minutes. Let's go!
[T circulates the room, checking student answers and assisting lower-performing students with locating paragraph references for question 3 and 4.]
L21 T: Transition & Pacing Time is up. Let's check our answers together. Question 1: Why does the writer love cooking? S5, please.
L22 S5: Because cooking is a great way for her to share her love with others, thanks to her grandmother.
L23 T: Encouragement & Feedback Perfect! Question 3: Where did the grandmother learn to make pumpkin pie? S6?
L24 S6: She learned it from a host family when she was a college student in the US.
L25 T: Encouragement & Feedback Wonderful job! 10 points for Group 5. Now, let's look at Activity 1d. Based on the Pumpkin Pie Recipe instructions, what is the correct order of the pictures?
[T points to the board where the picture letters D, B, C are written.]
Board Sequencing: 1d Recipe Pictures: [ D ] -> [ B ] -> [ C ] -> [ A ] -> [ E ]
L26 T: Instruction & Directives Let's do Activity 1e: Complete the summary of the text using key words from the reading. Fill in the blanks individually for two minutes.
[T gives students 2 minutes, then calls on S7 to read the completed summary.]
L27 T: CFU (Checks for Understanding) S7, read the first three blanks for us please.
L28 S7: Food connects people and creates memories. For example, I remember my grandmother and her pumpkin pie whenever I smell cinnamon.
L29 T: Encouragement & Feedback Spot on! Flawless reading and filling. 10 points for Group 6!
Step 4: Vocabulary in Use (27:00 - 34:00)
[27:00 - 30:00] Countable vs Uncountable Nouns (2a) | [30:00 - 34:00] Measure Words & Apple Pie Recipe Completion (2b, 2c)
L30 T: Transition & Pacing Now, let's turn to page 48, Vocabulary in Use. Let's look at Activity 2a. We need to categorize food and cooking words into vegetables, fruit, drinks, meat, snacks, and kitchen items, and decide if they are countable or uncountable nouns.
[T draws a two-column table on the blackboard: Countable Nouns vs. Uncountable Nouns.]
Board Table: Countable (e.g., potato, tomato, egg, bowl) | Uncountable (e.g., milk, sugar, salt, pepper, flour, beef, soup)
L31 T: Instruction & Directives Let's check Activity 2b: Match the pictures with the phrases. For example, "a bottle of milk", "a bowl of rice", "a glass of grape juice". These are measure words. S8, what is phrase number 4?
L32 S8: A piece of cake!
L33 T: Encouragement & Feedback Correct! And number 8? S9?
L34 S9: A spoon of sugar!
L35 T: Encouragement & Feedback Wonderful! Now let's complete Activity 2c: The Apple Pie recipe. Use the phrases in the box: add... to, pour... into, cut... into, share... with, mix... with. Work on this for two minutes.
[T monitors student writing, ensuring correct verb phrases are paired with ingredients.]
L36 T: CFU (Checks for Understanding) Let's check step 1 of the recipe. S10, what did you write?
L37 S10: First, cut the apples into pieces.
L38 T: Encouragement & Feedback Fantastic! 10 points for Group 3.
Step 5: Reading Integration & Writing Workshop (34:00 - 42:00)
[34:00 - 38:00] Guoqiao Rice Noodles Post Reading (3a) | [38:00 - 42:00] Writing a Response Post (3b, 3c)
L39 T: Transition & Pacing Let's turn to page 49. Look at Activity 3a: Reading the blog post "My Favourite Dish: Guoqiao Rice Noodles". This is a famous cultural dish from Yunnan Province, China. Let's circle the ingredients the chef uses as we read: vegetables, mushrooms, chicken, pork, chicken soup, rice noodles.
[T writes the writing scaffolding on the blackboard for Activity 3c.]
Board Writing Scaffold: Hi! My favourite dish is [dish name]. Here's how to make it. First, ... Next, ... Then, ... Finally, ...
L40 T: Instruction & Directives Now, look at Activity 3c on page 49. You need to write a response post about how to make your favourite dish. Use the adverbs of sequence we learned: First, Next, Then, After that, Finally. Write your short post in your exercise books. You have four minutes. Let's write!
[T walks around, providing individual spelling and grammar support, helping students structure their sentences correctly.]
L41 T: Transition & Pacing Time is up. Let's invite a volunteer to share their writing with the class. S11, please read your post.
L42 S11: Hi! My favourite dish is tomato egg noodles. Here's how to make it. First, boil some water and cook the noodles. Next, cut up the tomatoes and whisk two eggs. Then, fry the eggs and tomatoes in a pan. Finally, put the sauce on top of the noodles and mix them together!
L43 T: Encouragement & Feedback Oh, that sounds so delicious! Your use of adverbs of sequence is extremely clear and accurate. 20 points for Group 4! Excellent work!
Step 6: Summary, Reflection & Assignment (42:00 - 45:00)
[42:00 - 44:00] Reflecting on Learning Objectives | [44:00 - 45:00] Homework Assignment & Group PK Results
L44 T: Transition & Pacing Let's wrap up today's lesson by looking at the Reflecting section on page 50. Let's answer our Big Question: Why do we learn to cook? S12?
L45 S12: We learn to cook because cooking brings us happiness and love, and it connects us with our family and friends around the world.
L46 T: Encouragement & Feedback Spot on! Remember: Cooking brings us happiness and love.
[T calculates the final Group PK scores on the blackboard.]
Final Group PK Results: Group 1: 85 pts | Group 2: 80 pts | Group 3: 90 pts | Group 4: 95 pts (Winners!) | Group 5: 85 pts | Group 6: 90 pts
L47 T: Encouragement & Feedback Let's give a big round of applause to Group 4 for winning today's PK session! Great job, everyone!
[T writes homework assignment clearly on the blackboard.]
Homework: 1. Polish your food post in Activity 3c and share it with your parents. 2. Preview the Project on page 50: Create a recipe for a tasty dish with your group.
L48 T: Greeting & Hook That's all for today, class. Thank you for your hard work. Have a wonderful afternoon, and goodbye!
L49 Ss: Thank you, teacher! Goodbye!
[T smiles, packs up teaching materials, and dismisses the class.]

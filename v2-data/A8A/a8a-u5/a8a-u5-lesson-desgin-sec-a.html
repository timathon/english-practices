Unit 5 What a Delicious Meal! · Section A 教学设计

❓ BIG Question: Why do we learn to cook? / How do we make food?
课型: 听说与语法综合课
课时: 1 课时 (45分钟)
年级: 七年级 Grade 7
教材: Unit 5 Section A (pp. 41-45)
语言能力 (Language Ability) Master food/cooking vocabulary and sequence adverbs; accurately use exclamatory sentences (What/How) and imperative instructions.
文化意识 (Cultural Awareness) Appreciate Chinese and global culinary culture (e.g., stir-fried eggs and tomatoes), understanding food as a bridge connecting people.
思维品质 (Thinking Qualities) Develop logical and sequential thinking through analyzing cooking recipes and processes; enhance expressive force via exclamations.
学习能力 (Learning Ability) Actively engage in listening, peer collaboration, and task-based recipe creation; build autonomous vocabulary and grammar learning habits.
教学重难点 (Key & Difficult Points) & 教学方法:
  • 重点: Core cooking verbs (*cut up, mix, boil, bake, stir-fry*); sequence adverbs (*first, next, then, after that, finally*); Exclamatory sentence structures (*What a/an...!, How... !*).
  • 难点: 1) 特殊疑问句语序与疑问词选择 (*How many / How much*); 2) /eʊ/, /aʊ/, /ɪə/, /eə/, /ʊə/ 及辅音连缀 /sk, st, sp, str/ 的语音辨析与连读弱读; 3) 感叹句中 *What* (修饰名词/名词短语) 与 *How* (修饰形容词/副词) 的精准区分。
  • 方法与教具: Task-Based Language Teaching (TBLT), Communicative Language Teaching (CLT), Situational Approach; Multimedia, Flashcards, Audio recordings.

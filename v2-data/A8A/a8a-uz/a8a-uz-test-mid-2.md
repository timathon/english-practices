> Yunjin is a beautiful brocade(锦缎) made in Nanjing, Jiangsu Province. Yun in Chinese means clouds and jin means brocade. The materials used in yunjin are valuable, such as silk, gold and silver, resulting in its high collecting value.
>
> Yunjin, with a history of over 1, 600 years, has the best quality among all the brocades in China.lt stands for the finest Chinese silk cloth. The brocade comes out from a traditional wooden loom (织布机) as big as 5.6 meters long, 1.4 meters wide and 4 meters high. The huge loom needs two people to work, one above and one below. The process(过程) is so long and difficult that a brocade with a length of 5 to 6 centimeters takes two people all day to make and the production cannot be replaced with modern machines.
>
> Its pictures are large in shape and the use of gold and silver makes them rich and shining.In ancient times, yunjin served as materials for royal family's clothes and to beautify their palaces.It was also used as gifts for important foreign guests.
>
> With the development of technology and changes in people's lifestyle, young people have less interest in traditional arts which require a long time of learning and practicing. To pass down the skills of yunjin, the Chinese government set up Nanjing Yunjin Research Institute in 1957, which has helped spread the culture of brocade and built a cultural bridge between China and the world.

**D.**

# The Amazing Discovery of Plant Communication

> A recent study from the Chinese Academy of Sciences (中科院) has found a wonderful secret of the natural world; trees in a forest can actually "talk" to each other and work together to help each other stay healthy and grow.This discovery is changing our understanding of forests, showing they are far more than just many trees growing in one place.
>
> The study explains that trees are connected by a special web under the ground. This web is made of their roots and a kind of fungi (真菌) which acts like a natural internet, often called the "Wood Wide Web". When a tree is attacked by insects or is short of water, it doesn't stay in silence.Instead, it sends out special messages to tell other trees about the danger through this web.Nearby trees that receive these messages will quickly start making special things to protect themselves.
>
> What's more, the help doesn't stop there.In the forest, there are larger, older trees, called "mother trees", which play an important role. They are often the most connected centre in the web and will even share valuable nutrients (营养素) and water with younger trees through their roots and the fungi, giving them a better chance to grow better.
>
> This exciting discovery teaches us a very important lesson about nature.lt helps us understand why it's important to protect whole forest ecosystems, not just focusing on single trees. When we cut down too many trees, we break their communication and support webs. Then, they cannot help each other when there is a big storm, a tree sickness, or other problems like climate change. Life becomes much harder for them.
>
> The research team leader, Dr.Wang, shared his thoughts on the bigger lesson: "This teaches us a beautiful lesson about working together and community.Just like trees that support each other unconditionally, we humans should also work together to protect our environment.Every tree matters in a forest, just as every person matters in our society." This finding reminds us that we are part of an interconnected world, and our future depends on learning from the wisdom of nature.

Unit 4 Amazing Plants and Animals · Section B Classroom Delivery Script

❓ BIG Question: How are plants and animals connected with us?
Grade Level: Grade 8 English / 八年级英语
Class Duration: 45 minutes (1 class period)
Class Type: Reading, Vocabulary & Ecological Connection Analysis
Core Competencies: Comprehending informational texts about bees, extracting ecological mind maps, mastering word-formation suffixes (-ance, -(t)ion, -ness, -ity), exploring human-nature interdependence.
Standard Classroom English Badges Reference

Step 2: Pre-Reading & Fast Reading (07:00 - 18:00)
[07:00 - 11:00] 1a & 1b Adjective Underlining | [11:00 - 18:00] Paragraph-by-Paragraph Detailed Comprehension
L06 T: 🔴 Student Task Look at 1a and 1b on page 36. Look at the title "We Can't Live Without Bees" and the close-up photograph of honeybees on the honeycomb. Quickly read the text and underline all the adjectives the writer uses to describe bees. You have 3 minutes. Go!
[T walks around the classroom, monitoring student underlining work, checking spelling and adjective recognition.]
L07 T: 🟠 Transition & Pacing Time is up! Pens down, eyes on me. S2, what adjectives did you find in paragraph 2?

L10 S3: "Interesting"! Bees are also one of the most interesting animals in the world.

Step 3: Post-Reading & Mind Mapping (18:00 - 28:00)
[18:00 - 23:00] Completing 1c Mind Map | [23:00 - 28:00] Discussion & Critical Thinking (1d)
L13 T: 🔵 Instruction & Directives Now let's move to Activity 1c on page 37. Complete the mind map with information from the text. Let's check three branches: importance, problem, and interesting facts. Work in pairs for 4 minutes.
[T draws a blank mind map outline on the board labeled 'Bees' in the center, inviting students to come up and fill in branches.]
L14 T: 🟣 CFU - Checks for Understanding Group 1 representative, please come to the board and write down the 'importance' branch. S5, show us what you got.
[S5 walks up to the whiteboard and writes: 1. greatest work: pollination; 2. visit 50 to 100 flowers; 3. help plants grow / 80% of plants.]
L15 T: 🟡 Encouragement & Feedback Flawless writing and accurate details! 15 points for Group 1! Now, what about the 'problem' branch? S6?

📋 Blackboard Writing (Complete Mind Map):
[Bees Center]
├─ Importance: greatest work -> pollination | visit 50-100 flowers | help 80% plants grow

L17 T: 🟠 Transition & Pacing Excellent. Look at Activity 1d. Let's discuss question 1: Can you give another title for the text? Any creative ideas?

Step 4: Vocabulary in Use & Word Formation (28:00 - 36:00)
[28:00 - 32:00] 2a Odd-One-Out | [32:00 - 36:00] 2b Suffix Word Formation & 2c Sentence Completion
L20 T: 🔵 Instruction & Directives Let's turn to page 38, Vocabulary in Use. Look at Activity 2a. Circle the odd ones and give your reasons. Let's do number 1: habit, height, size, weight. Which one is the odd one out? S8?
L21 S8: "Habit" is the odd one out. Height, size, and weight are physical measurements, but habit is a behavior or routine.
L22 T: 🟡 Encouragement & Feedback Spot on! 10 points for Group 4! Now let's examine Activity 2b on page 38: adding suffixes *-ance*, *-(t)ion*, *-ness*, and *-ity* to form nouns. Let's write the noun forms on the board.
[T writes root words on the board and invites student volunteers to write the corresponding derived noun suffixes.]
📋 Blackboard Writing (Vocabulary Derivation Table):
act -> action (-tion) | good -> goodness (-ness)
appear -> appearance (-ance) | real -> reality (-ity)
connect -> connection (-tion) | important -> importance (-ance)
communicate -> communication (-tion)
L23 T: 🟣 CFU - Checks for Understanding Look at those patterns. Notice how verbs and adjectives transform into abstract nouns. Now, let's complete Activity 2c quickly. Number 1: "I felt ________ when my favourite flowers died." S9?
L24 S9: Disappointed. "I felt disappointed when my favourite flowers died."
L25 T: 🟡 Encouragement & Feedback Excellent. Perfect grammatical form.

L30 T: 🔴 Student Task Now look at Activity 3b. Imagine you are a blue whale named Billy. Use the first person to rewrite the first paragraph. Let's write a sample sentence structure on the board: "Hello everyone! My name is Billy...". Write your mini-paragraph in your notebooks. You have 2 minutes.

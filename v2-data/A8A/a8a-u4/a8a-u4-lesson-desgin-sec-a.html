Unit 4 Amazing Plants and Animals · Section A 教学设计与导学练

❓ BIG Question: What makes plants and animals amazing? / How do superlative adjectives help us describe extreme nature?
课型： 听说与语法综合课
时长： 1 课时 (45 分钟)
年级： 七年级 (Grade 7)
页码： Textbook P31–P35
语言能力 (Language Ability) Master core vocabulary (*moss, redwood, cheetah, bamboo, shoot, weigh*); use superlative adjectives and adverbs correctly to describe extreme attributes of plants and animals.
文化意识 (Cultural Awareness) Understand the cultural symbolism of bamboo in traditional Chinese paintings (*symbol of goodness*), fostering pride in Chinese culture and global ecological awareness.
思维品质 (Thinking Qualities) Develop critical and comparative thinking by analyzing extreme natural features, classifying plants/animals, and constructing logical mind maps.
学习能力 (Learning Ability) Enhance listening discrimination skills (stress, intonation, phonetic groups like /eɪ/, /bl/), and actively engage in pair work and role-play tasks.
📌 教学重点 (Focus Points) 1. Superlative forms of adjectives and adverbs (*the largest, the fastest, the most popular*).
2. Vocabulary describing plants and animals (*moss, redwood, bamboo, shoot*).
3. Listening for specific factual details in conversations.
⚠️ 教学难点 (Difficult Points) 1. Grammar: Irregular superlatives and multi-syllable superlative structures.
2. Phonetics: Consonant blends (*bl, cl, gl, br, cr, gr*) & sentence stress shifting meaning.
3. Culture: Expressing abstract cultural concepts (e.g., bamboo as a symbol of goodness) in English.

二、板书设计 (Blackboard Design Layout)

1. Core Inquiries

  • BIG Q: What makes plants & animals amazing?
  • Animals:
  • • Blue whale (largest/heaviest)
  • • Cheetah (fastest running)
  • Plants:
  • • Moss (oldest plant)
  • • Redwood (tallest tree)
  • • Bamboo (fastest-growing)

2. Grammar & Structure

  • Superlatives (最高级):
  • • Short adj: the + adj-est
    (large → the largest)
  • • Long adj: the most + adj
    (useful → the most useful)
  • • Adverb: runs the fastest
  • Culture: Bamboo = symbol of goodness

3. Culture & Group PK

  • Bamboo Sea (Sichuan):
  • • Largest & oldest forest
  • • Uses: build houses, tools, food (shoots)

Step 4: Grammar Focus & Practice - Section A 3a-3c (30:00 - 38:00)
[30:00 - 34:00] Discovering Grammar Rules (3a)
T (L27): 🟠 [Transition & Pacing] Let's turn to page 35: Grammar Focus. Look at the bold words in the sentences: the largest, the heaviest, the biggest, the fastest, the most useful, the most interesting.
[T writes the grammatical rules clearly on the blackboard: 1. Short adjectives: the + adj.-est (large -> largest). 2. Double final consonant: big -> biggest. 3. Long adjectives: the most + adj. (useful -> most useful). 4. Adverbs: runs the fastest.]
Board Writing: Grammar Box: Superlatives of Adjectives & Adverbs | 1. the + adj.-est | 2. double consonant + -est | 3. the most + adj. | 4. run the fastest
T (L28): 🟣 [CFU - Checks for Understanding] S7, tell me, how do we form the superlative of the short adjective "cute"?
S7: We add '-est': the cutest!
T (L29): 🟡 [Encouragement & Feedback] Excellent! Exactly right.
[34:00 - 38:00] Sentence Completion & Passage Filling (3b & 3c)

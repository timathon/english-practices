## 阅读理解(每小题3分,共15分)

Meng Xiang was crazy about films and music during his childhood. When he started college, he dreamed of making films that could combine (结合) his love for storytelling and interest in music.

His biggest challenge came when he planned to make a film about a tennis player. As no one would offer him money for a 2-hour film, Meng and his college friends tried to make a shorter one. They bought a second-hand camera and used old things instead of expensive equipment (设备). They even made a shelf on their own to support the camera.

Meng and his friends worked hard, rain or shine. When filming the tennis player, they ran after him in the summer sun. If it rained and they couldn't film outside, they hung bed sheets (床单) on the walls in an empty room to set up a simple studio (摄影棚). This 12-minute film got much attention. The teachers and students in their college liked the music they created. After that, Meng had another idea: to make a musical film. But musicals weren't popular in college, and many said it wouldn't work. Instead of listening to the doubts(怀疑), Meng and his team spent weeks writing new songs and making a test video. To film the moving scenes, they fixed(固定) a smartphone on a skateboard and pushed it. The actors' hair was blowing in the wind while they were dancing and singing. The loud voice and laughter of the team filled the air.

Their musical film became a hit and won several awards at a film festival. As Meng said, "Making films isn't about having the best equipment or famous actors. It's about finding new ways to tell your story. Our team is planning to make a special documentary about the environment. And I can't wait to write music for it."

## 任务型阅读(每小题2分,共10分)

阅读下面短文,根据短文内容,按要求完成下列各题。

As the saying goes, "If you fail to plan, you are planning to fail." Many people may think of planning as a boring thing, but it's actually one of the most useful skills we should learn. Planning means creating a timeline and a road map for achieving the goals we have set for ourselves.

In fact, we all plan in one way or another and enjoy the benefits (好处) of it in our daily lives, even if we don't realize it. A great example of this is when we plan our holidays. Planning helps us remember what to do and where to go before we leave. By planning ahead, we can enjoy a much better holiday experience.

Planning is also widely used for managing a personal budget. By making a spending plan, we can regularly check our finances (财力) and stop ourselves from wasting money on unnecessary things. This helps us save money and achieve both big dreams (like foreign travels) and small wishes (like weekly snacks).

For some people, planning has become an important part of life. Whether it's for studying, working, or achieving personal dreams, a good plan helps us use time wisely and avoid mistakes. Besides, having a plan enables us to deal with variables (易变的事), which can reduce stress compared to not having a plan at all.

Now you see why planning is so important! To get started, you can write down your goals, break them into easier-to-achieve parts, and then start planning how to make them come true. Little by little, your plans will turn your big dreams into real-life achievements!

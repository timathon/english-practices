# Listening Scripts

### Page 69, Fuel Up, Activity 3: Listen and Match
**Gary:** Welcome to London, Aunt Jane. Here's a map of the city. Let me show you some places.  
**Aunt Jane:** Thanks, Gary.  
**Gary:** First, from my home, go straight on and turn right. You can see a supermarket on your left.  
**Aunt Jane:** Okay, I can walk there.  
**Gary:** Yes. Next, go straight on. Turn left and turn right. Walk along TC Road. Then you can find a cinema.  
**Aunt Jane:** Got it. Can I walk to the British Museum from there?  
**Gary:** Yes. Go down TC Road, turn left, and go straight on. It's on your left.  
**Aunt Jane:** Great. How about the British Library?  
**Gary:** Well, you can go by bus. Take number 1.  
**Aunt Jane:** Thank you. Oh, how can I get home then?  
**Gary:** You can come back by bus, too. It's number 205.

**十四、写作。**

同学们,请运用“OREO框架”就以下话题来表达你的观点吧。

[*LAYOUT: Diagram with four stacked cookies labeled O, R, E, O*]
> # Is life better with technology?
> - **Step 1:** Show your opinions. (Opinion)
> - **Step 2:** Give your reasons. (Reason)
> - **Step 3:** Use examples to support your reasons. (Example)
> - **Step 4:** Write a summary(总结) of your opinions. (Opinion)


**十四、例文:**

> - • **Opinion:** I think life is better with technology.
> - • **Reason:** It brings us ease, skills and joy.
> - • **Example:** We can chat online and learn new things at home. It also brings us lots of fun.
> - • **Opinion:** In short, technology makes our lives wonderful.
> 
> I think life is better with technology. Because it brings us ease, skills and joy. For example, we can chat online and learn new things at home. Besides, it also brings us lots of fun. So in short, technology truly makes our lives wonderful.

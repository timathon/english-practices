**十四、写作。**

同学们，经过这个单元的学习，如果你有自己的小发明，或者曾经完成过一件印象深刻的小制作，就请你和同学们分享一下吧！

以下问题供你参考:
* What did you make?
* Why did you want to make it?
* What did you need to make it?
* How did you make it? (Try to tell the steps with "first, next, then, last...")
* What do you think of your idea or your work?


**十四、例文:**

> # A Small Invention
> 
> I had a small invention. I made a beautiful tissue bag because our class didn't have one. I needed an old takeaway bag. First, I cut off the top of the old bag. Next, I folded it into the right size. Then I tied it with strings. Last, I put a star on it. My tissue bag was not only beautiful, but also useful. I made it with old things, because I knew living a greener life was more meaningful. I think my idea is very creative. I love making things to solve small problems in daily life.

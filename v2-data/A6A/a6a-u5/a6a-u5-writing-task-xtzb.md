**十四、写作。**

同学们,保持身心健康十分重要。你为健康生活制订了哪些计划呢?请你以“My Healthy Plan”为题,写一篇短文。以下是一些提示问题,希望能帮助你构思:

- What will you eat?
- What will you drink?
- What will you do?
- When will you sleep?
- What do you think of your plan?

# My Healthy Plan


**十四、例文:**

> Keeping my body and mind healthy is very important. First, I will do enough exercise every day, such as running or playing sports. Second, I will eat healthy food like fruits and vegetables. Third, I will spend less time on screens and go to bed early. I will also spend time with my friends to stay happy. I hope my plan can help me stay healthy and strong.

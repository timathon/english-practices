**十四、写作。**

请你以“My Favourite Place to Travel”为题,参考提示问题,用不少于五句话介绍你最喜欢的旅行地。

1. What is your favourite place to travel?  
2. Why do you like it?  
3. What can you see there?  
4. What can you do there?  
5. How do you feel there?  

# My Favourite Place to Travel


**十四、例文:**

> My favourite place to travel is Dali. I like it because it has beautiful mountains and a big lake. There I can see the Erhai Lake and the old streets. I can ride a bike around the lake and take a walk on the old Tea Horse Road, The weather is also nice all year round. I hope to visit Dali again one day.

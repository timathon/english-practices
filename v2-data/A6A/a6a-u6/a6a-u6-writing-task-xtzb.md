同学们,西班牙的番茄大战节是不是很有趣?在中国和世界上其他地方还有许多有趣的节日,比如Christmas(圣诞节)、Water-splashing Festival(泼水节)和 Torch Festival(火把节)等。想象一下,如果你有机会参加其中一个节日,你会选择哪个呢?请你以“A Festival I Want to Join in”为题,写一段不少于五句话的短文,描述你想参加的节日以及你的心情。下面的问题可以帮助你构思:

1. Which festival do you want to join in?  
2. What do people do at this festival?  
3. What will you wear or bring?  
4. How do you feel about it?  
5. What makes this festival special?  

# A Festival I Want to Join in


**十四、例文:**

> I want to join in the Water-splashing Festival in Yunnan, China. During the festival, people splash water on each other to wish for good luck. I will wear a swimsuit and bring a bucket. I feel very excited because it's like a big water fight. I think this festival is special because it brings people together and washes away bad luck.

> # Gift-giving Customs Around the World
> Gift-giving customs are different around the world. In China, it is polite to refuse (拒绝) a gift two or three times before accepting it. This shows modesty (谦逊). In Japan, people use both hands to give and receive gifts. They do not open gifts in front of the giver. In Western countries like the US and the UK, people usually open gifts right away and say "Thank you". In India, gifts are often given with the right hand only. The left hand is considered (认为) unclean. In many countries, giving a clock as a gift is unlucky because it sounds like "attending a funeral”(出席葬礼)in some languages. Learning these customs can help you avoid embarrassment (尴尬) and show respect.

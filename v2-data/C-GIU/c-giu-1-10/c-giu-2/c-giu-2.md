**Unit 2**

# Present simple (I do)

**A** Study this example situation:

[*VISUAL: Alex is asleep in his bed with an alarm clock nearby, dreaming or showing a thought bubble of driving a city bus.*]

Alex is a bus driver, but now he is in bed asleep.[cite: 2]
He is not driving a bus.[cite: 2] (He is asleep.)[cite: 2]
*but* **He drives** a bus.[cite: 2] He is a bus driver.[cite: 2]

**drive(s)**, **work(s)**, **do(es)** etc. is the present simple:[cite: 2]

| Subject | Base / -s Form |
| :--- | :--- |
| **I / we / you / they** | **drive / work / do** etc.[cite: 2] |
| **he / she / it** | **drives / works / does** etc.[cite: 2] |

---

**B** We use the present simple to talk about things in general.[cite: 2] We use it to say that something happens all the time or repeatedly, or that something is true in general:[cite: 2]

* Nurses **look after** patients in hospitals.[cite: 2]
* I usually **go** away at weekends.[cite: 2]
* The earth **goes** round the sun.[cite: 2]
* The cafe **opens** at 7.30 in the morning.[cite: 2]

We say:
* I **work** *but* he **works**[cite: 2]
* you **go** *but* it **goes**[cite: 2]
* they **teach** *but* my sister **teaches**[cite: 2]
* I **have** *but* he **has**[cite: 2]

For spelling (-s or -es), see Appendix 6.[cite: 2]

---

**C** We use **do/does** to make questions and negative sentences:[cite: 2]

| Questions | | | Negatives | | |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **do** | **I / we / you / they** | **work?**[cite: 2] | **I / we / you / they** | **don't** | **work**[cite: 2] |
| | | **drive?**[cite: 2] | | | **drive**[cite: 2] |
| **does** | **he / she / it** | **do?**[cite: 2] | **he / she / it** | **doesn't** | **do**[cite: 2] |

* I come from Canada. Where **do you come** from?[cite: 2]
* I **don't go** away very often.[cite: 2]
* What **does this word mean**? (*not* What means this word?)[cite: 2]
* Rice **doesn't grow** in cold climates.[cite: 2]

In the following examples, **do** is also the main verb (**do you do / doesn't do** etc.):[cite: 2]
* 'What **do you do**?' 'I work in a shop.'[cite: 2]
* He's always so lazy. He **doesn't do** anything to help.[cite: 2]

---

**D** We use the present simple to say how often we do things:[cite: 2]

* I **get up** at 8 o'clock every morning.[cite: 2]
* How often **do you go** to the dentist?[cite: 2]
* Julie **doesn't drink** tea very often.[cite: 2]
* Robert usually **goes** away two or three times a year.[cite: 2]

---

**E** **I promise / I apologise** etc.

Sometimes we do things by saying something.[cite: 2] For example, when you promise to do something, you can say '**I promise...**'; when you suggest something, you can say '**I suggest...**':[cite: 2]

* **I promise** I won't be late. (*not* I'm promising)[cite: 2]
* 'What do you suggest I do?' '**I suggest** that you ...'[cite: 2]

In the same way we say: **I apologise** ... / **I advise** ... / **I insist** ... / **I agree** ... / **I refuse** etc.[cite: 2]

# Exercises

**2.1** Complete the sentences using the following verbs:[cite: 2]

> **cause(s)** | **close(s)** | **connect(s)** | **go(es)** | **live(s)** | **speak(s)** | **take(s)**[cite: 2]

2. Ben and Jack ... to the same school.[cite: 2]

5. My parents ... in a very small flat.[cite: 2]

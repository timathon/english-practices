1. Vision & AI-Scaffolded Pedagogy Core Blueprint

Traditional classical Chinese learning relies heavily on repetitive rote recitation (死记硬背), which leaves children detached from historical meaning. 白莲阁 (Bái Lián Gé) within the 知新堂 platform integrates an "Invisible AI Teacher" to shift learning from forced memorization to deep, intrinsic cultural appreciation.

Micro-Feedback & Visual Reward Loops

Short attention spans are anchored through instant visual feedback: ink-stroke reveals, character radical unlocks, and glowing silhouette assembly rather than standard score screens.

Adaptive Vygotsky Scaffolding

The platform automatically tunes prompt difficulty based on real-time performance. High accuracy peels away pinyin and hints; difficulty spikes dynamically to maintain the optimal "Zone of Proximal Development".

Spaced Repetition (SM-2 AI Scheduler)

Mastered verses reappear in quick daily micro-reviews right before forgetting occurs according to individual memory curves, ensuring durable recall with minimal effort.

Invisible AI Pedagogy

No clumsy conversational chatbots. The AI operates silently: adjusting choices, generating age-appropriate explanations, creating contextual hints, and distilling class metrics into actionable teaching insights.

Daily Practice & Accuracy 智慧点 (Points) Engine

To encourage daily practice consistency and reward high accuracy while strictly preventing point-farming tricks:

⏱️ 1. Timely Submission (+10 pts)

Awarded only on the very first attempt of an assignment if submitted on or before the deadline date.

📅 2. Base Daily Completion (首刷20 / 次日10)

Awarded unconditionally +20 pts on 1st attempt ever. On subsequent days, awarded +10 pts max once/day only if today's score ≥ historical best score.

🎯 3. Historical Accuracy Scale
  • • 100% Accuracy: 25 pts
  • • 90% – 99%: 20 pts
  • • 80% – 89%: 15 pts
  • • 70% – 79%: 5 pts
  • • <70%: 0 pts

Retries award the differential when exceeding all-time historical best tier.

🔒 100% Daily Lock Rule: Scoring 100% accuracy locks the assignment for retries until the next day ("已满分完成！明天可再练习").

4. Student Frontend & Adaptive Engine

The frontend UI balances engaging visuals with deep cognitive adaptation. As children answer questions, the engine adjusts difficulty in real time to maintain a flow state.

Real-Time Adaptive Difficulty Mechanics

⚡ High Accuracy (5 consecutive correct)
  • Strips away Pinyin guidance.
  • Reduces available visual hint options.
  • Upgrades prompt from multiple-choice to scrambled line assembly.
🛡️ Struggle Detection (2 consecutive errors)
  • Injects partial Pinyin and image clue cards.
  • Reduces distractor choice counts from 4 to 2 options.
  • Triggers automated AI acoustic audio hints for pronunciation.


## **Project Name: 知新堂 (Zhī Xīn Táng)**

*Next-Generation Gamified Homework & Classroom Review Platform for K-6 Students*

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**知新堂 (Zhī Xīn Táng)** is an Eastern Sci-Fi / Solarpunk educational web platform designed for primary students (ages 6–12). Rooted in the classic philosophy **“温故而知新”** (*Reviewing the old to discover the new*), **知新堂** bridges the gap between classroom teaching and home practice by turning **teacher assignments (作业)** and **after-class review (课后复习)** across **Chinese (语文: 识字, 拼音, 古诗, 成语)**, **Math (数学)**, and **English (英语)** into an epic story of world restoration.

### **The Core Narrative & Strategy**

**知新堂** is an ancient, high-tech Sanctuary Hall of Learning that bridges teachers, students, and the Earth below:

* **"温故" (Completing & Reviewing):** Students enter **知新堂** to complete teacher-assigned homework scrolls and review classroom knowledge. Every correct answer generates the energy needed to power the Sanctuary.
* **"知新" (Innovating & Saving Earth):** Using this energy, scholars awaken as **“知新使者” (Zhixin Emissaries)**. They craft high-tech Eco-Artifacts, hatch companion spirit beasts, and solve global ecological and cosmic crises.

## 2. Brand Identity & The "温故而知新" Loop

The name **知新堂** naturally connects school assignments to immersive gameplay:

```
                  ┌──► 温 故 (Teacher Assignments & Review)
                  │    • Completing daily teacher homework scrolls (堂课作业 / 师门令)
  知 新 堂 GAME    │    • Reviewing classroom mistakes & consolidating knowledge (错题复盘 / 错题本)
    MAIN LOOP ────┤    • Practicing Chinese (识字, 拼音, 古诗, 成语), Math, and English
                  │──► 知 新 (Application & Discovery)
                  │    • Unlocking high-tech Eco-Artifacts (知新宝器) & Star Gems (知新星石)
                  └    • Solving real-world eco-crises & cosmic threats
```

### **The Four Chambers of 知新堂 (Architecture & Built-in Modules)**

1. **正堂 (The Main Hall / Homework Center):** Receiving and completing daily teacher assignments (作业中心 / 师门令), featuring multi-subject quiz engines for Chinese, Math, and English with auto-grading.
2. **温故室 (The Review Chamber):** Self-paced review of core subjects, personal Error Log (错题本 / 温故手册), and practice sub-sections for **Chinese (识字, 拼音, 古诗, 成语)**, **Math**, and **English**.
3. **知新坊 (The Innovation Lab & Merchant Pavilion):** Avatar customization, room decor, Star Gems (知新星石) rewards shop, and crafting gear/spirit beasts.
4. **观星台 (The Observatory):** Sanctuary status display, points analytics, and real-time global crisis map tracking Earth pollution & incoming cosmic threats.

## 4. Existing Base vs. Phase 1 Integration

### **What We Already Have in `zxt` Codebase:**
- **Multi-Subject Quiz Engine:** Framework supporting quiz questions, multiple choice, word unscrambling, audio SFX, and history tracking.
- **Teacher Assignment Workflow:** Teachers can select subject content, set due dates, assign custom requirements, and track class submission rates & detailed statistics.
- **Student Dashboard:** Tabs for current assignments, quiz execution modal, quiz history details, and self-study exploration.
- **Question Editor & Admin Panel:** Platform tools for managing questions, quiz items, users, and class rosters.
- **Points & Currency System (知新星石):** Points logged on homework completion, viewable in Points History modals.

### **Preservation Commitment:**
All underlying engines—including assignment publishing, teacher analytics, question editors, quiz execution modals, and user authentication—remain strictly intact as foundational components within **正堂** and **温故室**.

## 5. Phased Product Roadmap

```
[ Phase 1: MVP Launch ] ──► [ Phase 2: Season 1 ] ──► [ Phase 3: Season 2 ] ──► [ Phase 4: Season 3 ]
   The Peaceful Hall           Crisis Awakening           Spirit Beasts               Classroom Co-op
   Assignments & 4 Chambers    Eco-Crises & Maps          Pet Hatching & Passives     Asteroid Interception
```

### **Phase 1: "The Peaceful Hall" (Current Target)**

* **Focus:** Consolidating teacher assignment engine, student 4-Chamber portal navigation, review mode (Chinese sub-sections: **识字, 拼音, 古诗, 成语**, Math & English error logs), modular Avatar Creator (**使者形象/Avatar Customization**), and **知新星石 (Star Gems)** avatar/room rewards shop.
* **Features:**
  * **Unified 4-Chamber Hub Navigation:** Seamless navigation between **正堂 (Assignments)**, **温故室 (Review & Core Subjects)**, **知新坊 (Innovation, Avatar Creator & Star Gem Shop)**, and **观星台 (Observatory & Stats)**.
  * **Interactive Avatar Creator (使者形象/Avatar Studio):** In **知新坊**, students can choose and compose their custom **“知新使者” (Zhī Xīn Emissary)** avatar (selecting hairstyle/face, scholar robes/outfits, modern accessories, and background halo). Unlocked items can be equipped dynamically and will display across the student profile, top navbar, and homework completion victory screens.
  * **Teacher Assignment Dashboard:** Assign daily practice for Chinese (识字, 拼音, 古诗, 成语), Math, and English aligned with primary school textbooks.
  * **Student Portal & Quiz Engine:** Complete homework scrolls, review error logs, earn Star Gems with real-time audio/visual rewards.
  * **知新星石 (Star Gems) Shop:** Spend earned gems on unlocking new scholar robes, hairstyles, modern accessories, and personal room decorations inside *知新堂*.


### **Phase 2: "The Earth Awakening" (Season 1 Expansion)**
* **Focus:** Environmental crises hit the Earth below; homework completion fuels world restoration.
* **Features:** Global Crisis Map (*Plastic Coast*, *Smog Valley*), **知新宝器 (Eco-Artifacts)**, screen purification engines.

### **Phase 3: "Wildlife & Spirit Beasts" (Season 2 Expansion)**
* **Focus:** Rescuing endangered species and hatching companions (**阁中灵兽**).

### **Phase 4: "Cosmic Interception" (Season 3 Expansion)**
* **Focus:** Class-wide co-op events against high-stakes threats (e.g., asteroid collisions).

## 8. Business Model & Monetization Strategy

1. **B2B School / Teacher Adoption (Freemium):** Free core assignment and grading tools for teachers and schools, driving rapid student onboarding.
2. **B2C Parent Subscription:** Optional premium pass for parents providing detailed learning analytics, exclusive avatar cosmetics, and seasonal lore side-quests.
3. **Strict Non-Pay-to-Win Policy:** Academic performance and homework completion are the **only** ways to progress in the game—gear and abilities cannot be bought with real money.

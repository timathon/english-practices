The book uses a consistent **4-page modular unit** for each idiom solitaire group, taking learners through sequential recognition, semantic understanding, and narrative context:

* **Modular 4-Page Unit Structure (per group):**
* **Page 1 (Visual Solitaire Chain):** Introduces 16 connected four-character idioms (*chéngyǔ*) mapped into a winding visual pathway, where each idiom begins with the ending character of the previous one. All characters include Pinyin above the text.


* **Page 2 (Definitions & Reference Table):** Formats all 16 idioms into a reference table providing concise definitions and historical contexts for each phrase in corresponding sequential order.


* **Pages 3 & 4 (Illustrated Idiom Stories):** Selects key anchor idioms from the chain (e.g., the first two: *一字千金* and *叶公好龙*) and dedicates a full page to each. These pages feature full Pinyin-annotated origin stories accompanied by classical or cartoon-style illustrations.




* **Overall Book Organization (33 Groups):**
* **Scale:** Spanning 33 groups across 132 pages ($33 \times 4$), covering 528 idioms in total ($33 \times 16$) and 66 featured story breakdowns.
* **Pedagogical Flow:** Employs a three-step learning methodology across every module: **Mnemonic Linking** (solitaire chain on Page 1) $\rightarrow$ **Lexical Comprehension** (definitions on Page 2) $\rightarrow$ **Cultural & Narrative Context** (origin stories on Pages 3–4).
